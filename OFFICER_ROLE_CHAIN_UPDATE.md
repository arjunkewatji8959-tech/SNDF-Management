# Officer Role / Chain Update

SNDF hierarchy is now:

Master Admin → Admin → Field Officer → Officer → Supervisor → Guard

- Officer has a dedicated `officer.html` dashboard based on the Supervisor dashboard.
- Field Officer can create Officers under their own Staff ID.
- Officer can create Supervisors under their own Staff ID.
- Supervisor creates Guards under the existing Guard workflow.
- Officer team attendance shows Supervisors and their Guards.
- Officer is available as a login role.
- Point transfer supports Officer as well.
