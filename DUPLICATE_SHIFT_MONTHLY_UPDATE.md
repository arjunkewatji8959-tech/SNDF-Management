# Monthly Attendance – Double Shift Update

Monthly attendance now keeps multiple attendance records for the same staff member on the same date.

- One shift on a date: `P`
- Two completed shifts on the same date: `P × 2`
- The cell also shows the two shift names, e.g. `Morning + Evening`.
- Duty Count counts completed attendance units (Half Day = 0.5).
- Shift Count counts completed shift records.
- CSV export continues to output each shift as a separate row, so double-shift data is never merged or lost.
